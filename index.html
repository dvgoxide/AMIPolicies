Arena Match Info
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Arena Match Info (“the Extension,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use its Twitch desktop or mobile views and its companion World of Warcraft combat-log reader and uploader.

The Extension is operated by Core Zero Games. Privacy contact: corezerogames@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

Arena and combat information. When a broadcaster runs the companion reader, it reads World of Warcraft combat logs selected on that broadcaster’s computer. Parsed information can include character names and realms, character and combat-unit identifiers, classes or specializations, teams and rosters, arena brackets and maps, match and round timestamps, outcomes, ratings and matchmaking ratings when available, and combat events such as abilities, damage, healing, auras, interrupts, dispels, and deaths. This includes information about other players present in the broadcaster’s recorded matches.

Published match information. The companion uploader sends parsed match snapshots, the active character, and a source log filename to the hosted backend. The current uploader does not upload the original raw combat-log text file. Parsed recaps can nevertheless contain detailed combat-event information from that file.

Twitch information. Twitch supplies authorization information, including a signed token, channel identifier, viewer role, and a Twitch-issued viewer identifier. Depending on Twitch permissions, a token may also include a linked Twitch account identifier. We use authorization information to verify requests and provide the appropriate channel’s statistics. The current backend does not create a database of viewer profiles or store viewer authorization tokens in its application database. The Extension does not ask you to enter your Twitch password.

Technical and support information. Twitch and our hosting provider, Render, process network requests and may process IP addresses, request times, device or browser information, and operational or security logs. If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address and message.

2. How we use information

We use information to display arena statistics and combat recaps; associate published data with the broadcaster’s Twitch channel; authenticate requests; maintain and troubleshoot the service; protect against misuse; and respond to support or privacy requests. We do not sell personal information or use Extension data for targeted advertising.

3. What viewers can see and who receives information

Match snapshots are intended for display to the broadcaster’s Twitch audience. Viewers who can access the Extension may see information about the broadcaster and other match participants, including character names, teams, results, ratings when available, and combat recaps. Treat uploaded match information as information shared with the channel’s audience. Viewers may copy or capture what they see.

Twitch provides the Extension platform, authorization, frontend hosting, and channel configuration services. Render hosts the backend that receives and serves published match snapshots. These providers process information to operate their services and under their own policies. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to protect the service or others.

Provider policies: Twitch Privacy Notice and Render Privacy Policy.

4. Storage and retention

The broadcaster’s raw logs and local match-history database remain on the broadcaster’s computer unless the broadcaster separately shares them. The current reader has no automatic time-based deletion of local match history.

The current uploader publishes up to the latest 2,000 parsed match records and may omit older detailed recaps to fit the upload size limit. The backend stores the latest published snapshot for the channel and replaces it when a new snapshot arrives. That snapshot can contain older matches; replacement alone does not mean every older match is removed. There is currently no automatic time-based expiration of the stored snapshot. Stopping the reader or uploader does not itself erase already published data.

Hosted snapshots remain until replaced, removed by the operator, or lost through hosting storage changes. Hosting logs and any provider-managed backups follow the provider’s retention practices. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request and related operational or legal obligations.

5. Cookies, analytics, and security

The current Extension code does not add advertising trackers, independent analytics scripts, or its own cookies or browser local-storage records. Twitch and hosting services may use their own technologies under their policies. The broadcaster’s backend connection setting is stored using Twitch’s configuration service.

The service uses HTTPS connections and request authentication to protect hosted data. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

Viewers can close or stop using the Extension. Broadcasters can stop future uploads by stopping the companion uploader and can disable the Extension on their channel. Local logs and match history must be managed separately on the broadcaster’s computer.

To ask about information associated with you, report an error, or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact corezerogames@gmail.com. Include the Twitch channel and relevant character name, realm, and approximate match date when possible. We may need reasonable information to verify and locate a request; do not send passwords or authorization tokens. We will consider requests under applicable law and explain any limitations. We cannot remove copies independently saved by viewers or data separately controlled by Twitch, Blizzard, or other services.

7. Children and international processing

The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Contact us if you believe such information has been provided so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Extension or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. We will provide additional notice where required by applicable law.

Privacy questions: corezerogames@gmail.com.
